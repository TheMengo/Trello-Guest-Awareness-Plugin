TRELLO GUEST AWARENESS

Know who has access.

A small reminder before you comment on a board with Workspace guests.

01

Awareness

A guest is a board member who is not a member of that board’s Trello Workspace. This does not establish whether someone works for your company.

02

Read-only Trello access

The extension needs your authorization to compare board and Workspace membership. Until access is verified, Trello shows “Unable to verify guest status.”

  1. Create or obtain your organization’s Trello API key using Trello’s developer portal. No API secret is needed.
  2. Enter the key below, choose a token expiration, then open the read-only authorization page. Review Trello’s permission screen and copy the token it provides.
  3. Paste the token, then save and verify access. Each person must authorize their own token; never share tokens.

03

Comment confirmations

Board and card dismissals are independent. Guest membership or relevant card signals changing brings the confirmation back. The small board indicator stays visible.

Awareness, without monitoring. Comment text is inspected transiently in the page to identify mentions. It is never saved or transmitted by this extension. No analytics, permissions changes, or comment posting API.
